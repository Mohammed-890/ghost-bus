          How a bus becomes a ghost

          1. The timetable. Every trip operators have registered with the Bus Open Data Service for the day.
          2. The buses. Every bus in England reports its position to the same service. We record Sheffield’s every minute, around the clock.
          3. Matching. A trip ran if a bus reported that exact trip, or if a bus with no trip ID passed the trip’s stops in order, on time. One bus can’t run two trips at once.
          4. Fairness. A trip is only called a ghost if the feed was answering for the whole trip and the operator was visibly reporting its other buses at the time. Otherwise it is not judged, never counted against anyone.
